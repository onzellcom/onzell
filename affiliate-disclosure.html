Affiliate Disclosure

How OnZell explains advertising, affiliate links and editorial independence.

Last updated: November 23, 2026.

Our relationship with merchants

OnZell publishes lifestyle inspiration and plans to feature products, services and travel experiences from independent merchants. In the future, some links may be affiliate links. If you click an affiliate link and make an eligible purchase or booking, OnZell may receive a commission, at no additional charge to you.

Current status: The introductory guides on this website are editorial content, not active merchant offers. We have not connected live affiliate product feeds in the current public version. Articles or pages containing affiliate recommendations will include a clear disclosure close to the recommendation or links when those partnerships go live.

Sponsored content

We will identify sponsored placements or paid collaborations distinctly from independent editorial material. Compensation should not be treated as a guarantee of a positive review or endorsement. We intend to distinguish editorial selections from paid placement.

Prices and availability

When product listings become available, displayed prices, availability and merchant terms may change. Confirm all details on the merchant's own website before purchasing. OnZell generally will not be the seller of products linked from third-party merchants.

Restaurant, family and wellness content

Our Discover & Enjoy section currently includes informational guides and outbound links to general news, weather and maps services; those links are not represented as affiliate partnerships. If we later add sponsored recipes, toys, dining recommendations or health-related products, material commercial relationships will be disclosed clearly and close to the relevant recommendation, not only on this page.

Questions

Contact contact@onzell.com with questions about a merchant relationship or disclosure.
