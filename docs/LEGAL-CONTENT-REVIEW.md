# OnZell Content and Legal Readiness Review
Reviewed: 2026-11-23

## Review scope
- Privacy Policy, Terms, Affiliate Disclosure and homepage disclosure language
- New family entertainment, child-oriented toy ideas, recipes, wellness tips, news/weather links and restaurant finder
- Current static website behavior (no OnZell accounts, analytics dashboard, working server forms or affiliate feeds in the repository)

## Changes applied
- Privacy notice explains voluntary Google Maps searches, third-party news/weather providers and absence of OnZell browser geolocation collection.
- Policies explain that child/family activities are general-audience editorial content, not targeted child accounts or individualized advice.
- Terms clarify toy supervision, food allergies, health-information limitations and restaurant provider responsibility.
- Affiliate disclosure makes clear that new editorial/third-party links are not paid endorsements and future paid recommendations need clear in-context labels.
- Current contact form only opens a visitor's email client; do not imply server receipt.
- No real-time proprietary news/weather feed is represented as already active; outbound links go to respective publishers/forecast services.

## Human/business verification required
- Confirm actual hosting services, analytics scripts, image CDNs, email-provider practices, logs and retention with account owners.
- Confirm whether OnZell meets applicable CCPA/CPRA coverage thresholds, and whether additional California privacy rights/notice methods are required; consult counsel.
- Validate company legal name, address, email and any brand or image licenses.
- Review children’s privacy implications before any account, user-generated story upload, child profile, audience-targeting advertising or tracking is introduced.
- Review FDA/FTC and other laws before adding health-product claims, endorsements or sponsored advice.
- If OnZell launches online subscriptions, add unsubscribe and consent rules and update privacy pages.
- When affiliate links begin, disclose commissions clearly beside each relevant offer and ensure terms/merchant approvals are current.
- Confirm any news/weather API commercial licensing before embedding feeds; links to independent websites do not grant republication rights.

This is an operational content review, NOT a formal legal opinion or a claim of compliance with every applicable law.
