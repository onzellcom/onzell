A weekend garden refresh

Make patios, planters and small outdoor corners feel more inviting.

Start with your conditions

Before choosing plants, notice how many hours of sunlight your space receives, whether it is windy and how quickly pots dry out. Plant labels and local garden centers can help you find varieties suited to your climate.

For a small balcony, a few healthy containers are often more pleasing than a crowded collection that is hard to maintain.

Create one beautiful focal point

A grouped trio of planters, a clean seating area or an uncluttered path can anchor the whole space. Repeat one or two colors, vary leaf texture and leave enough room for plants to grow.

If you garden with children or pets, check plant toxicity and avoid placing sharp tools or fertilizers where they can be reached.

Keep maintenance realistic

Use suitable containers with drainage, water according to the plant's needs and adjust with the season. The easiest garden to enjoy is the one you can care for consistently.
