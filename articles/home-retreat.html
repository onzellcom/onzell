Five ways to make home feel like vacation

Simple design choices for a space that helps you slow down and feel at ease.

Let light do the work

Open the curtains, clean the windows and keep the areas nearest natural light uncluttered. Soft lighting in the evening changes the mood of a room without requiring expensive furniture.

Layer a table lamp or warm accent light with your existing ceiling fixture so the space can shift from busy daytime to a calmer evening.

Choose one comfort ritual

A fresh set of towels, a comfortable chair, a small vase or a favorite tea can turn an ordinary routine into something restorative. Aim for changes you will enjoy repeatedly, not accessories that only look good in a photograph.

Natural-looking textures—linen, cotton, wood and woven materials—can add warmth. Choose durable, easy-care materials for high-use spaces.

Make a quiet corner

Find a small part of your home that can remain free of clutter and notifications. Add a comfortable seat, a book or plant and a gentle light. This is a place to reset, even if you only have ten minutes.

A retreat-inspired home should fit your actual life. Start with what you already own before shopping.
