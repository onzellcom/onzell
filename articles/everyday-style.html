Everyday style, thoughtfully

A smaller, more useful wardrobe can make getting dressed easier.

Begin with what you wear

Look at the pieces you reach for each week. They reveal your actual preferences more clearly than fleeting trends. Note the colors, fits and fabrics that feel comfortable and work across your routine.

Create a simple foundation with versatile layers and footwear appropriate for your weather and activities.

Look for combinations

Before buying anything new, imagine three outfits using pieces you already own. A color that repeats across your wardrobe often gives a new garment more versatility.

Fit, care instructions and comfort matter more than a promotional tag. Consider repair and tailoring before replacement.

Choose personal details

A scarf, meaningful jewelry or a favorite color can make a practical outfit feel individual. Fashion is most enjoyable when it supports how you live, rather than creating extra work.
