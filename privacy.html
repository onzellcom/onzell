Privacy Policy

What information OnZell currently handles and how to contact us about privacy.

Last updated: November 23, 2026.

This policy describes the current introductory OnZell website. It will be reviewed and updated as newsletter, accounts, analytics or affiliate integrations are activated.

Who we are

OnZell Corp, 16060 Ventura Blvd, Ste 110, Encino, CA 91436, United States. Privacy inquiries: contact@onzell.com.

Information you provide

If you contact us by email or use the site's email-compose form, you may provide your name, email address and the information included in your message. The current website opens your own email application for contact submissions; the website does not independently confirm delivery or store those form entries in a deployed OnZell database.

Our newsletter is not currently accepting online subscriptions. We will provide appropriate notice and consent choices before launching automated email subscriptions.

Automatic technical information

Our hosting, domain and email service providers may process technical information such as IP addresses, browser information, request logs, security signals and timestamps to deliver the site, prevent abuse and operate their services. Our current static frontend does not contain a connected OnZell advertising tracker or analytics account, but hosting providers may use essential operational technologies. The website may load images from an external image host, which may receive ordinary browser request information.

How information is used

We use inquiries to respond, evaluate potential partnerships, operate and secure the website, and comply with applicable legal obligations. We do not use the current form to enroll you automatically in a mailing list. We do not intentionally request sensitive financial, government identification or medical details through the contact form; please do not send them.

Service providers and external links

Our site uses hosting, email and potentially image delivery services. A link to a third-party website is governed by that site's own policies. If affiliate tracking is introduced later, we will describe relevant practices and disclosures before activation.

Retention and choices

We retain business correspondence as reasonably needed for the purpose of the inquiry, operational records and applicable obligations. You may contact us to request access, correction or deletion of personal information, subject to applicable law and verification. We will assess privacy requests under the laws that apply to OnZell and the requester.

Children

This website is intended for a general audience. We do not intentionally solicit personal information directly from children under 13; parents or guardians may contact us with concerns.

Discover & Enjoy, news, weather and restaurant links

Our Discover & Enjoy section offers original family-friendly articles, recipes, general wellness information and outbound links to third-party news, weather and maps services. We do not automatically request browser geolocation or store a location profile. If you type a city into the restaurant finder, the city remains in your browser until you choose to submit; your browser then opens a Google Maps search containing that city. If you use “restaurants near me,” Google Maps may ask for location permission under its own policies. We do not receive the location search result.

Weather and news links open the respective providers in a new tab. Those providers may collect information according to their own privacy notices. Embedded on-site real-time feeds are not currently active. Our family-friendly content is meant to be used by adults and families; we do not offer child accounts, direct child messaging or child-directed data collection.

California privacy rights

Depending on the nature and scale of our operations and applicable law, certain privacy rights and notices may apply to California residents and other visitors. You may contact us at contact@onzell.com to ask what personal information we hold, request correction or deletion, or raise a concern. We will verify and respond to requests as required by applicable law. We do not currently offer an OnZell advertising tracker, behavioral advertising program or online sale of visitor information, based on the features in this static frontend. This statement must be revisited before any new advertising, analytics, or affiliate-tracking technology is enabled.

Updates

We may update this policy when services change. The date above reflects the current version. Contact contact@onzell.com for questions.
