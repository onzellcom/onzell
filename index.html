November 26, 2026 · Home

Make a landing spot for the things you always lose

Stand where you usually enter your home and notice what comes out of your pockets first. Put a small dish or tray within reach for keys and earbuds, and leave space beside it for anything you need to take out tomorrow. A saucer you already own works fine.

Try it for three days. If your keys still end up on the kitchen counter, move the dish there. The best spot is the one your hand already reaches for.
