About OnZell

A lifestyle magazine for thoughtful discovery, ideas and beautiful finds.

Everything good is OnZell.

OnZell is an independent lifestyle discovery project operated by OnZell Corp, based in California. We're creating a warm, easy-to-browse place for travel inspiration, fragrance, style, home décor, gardening, kitchen ideas, family life and practical technology.

Our editorial approach

We want our stories to help people make considered choices, rather than feel pressured by a countdown or exaggerated deal. We currently publish original introductory guides and curated reading lists. Our public site is an early-stage magazine, and the product discovery marketplace is still under development.

How we expect to support the site

We plan to participate in affiliate programs with approved merchants. If a reader clicks a qualifying affiliate link and makes a purchase, we may earn a commission. We will clearly identify relevant commercial relationships. The presence of a brand in an article does not mean we currently have a partnership with it.
